How It Started

From Passion to Purpose

EMERALD was founded in 2019 by two fashion-obsessed friends frustrated by the choice between cheap fast fashion and unaffordable luxury. We believed there was a better way.

Today, EMERALD ships to 35+ countries and has dressed thousands of people for their most important moments — all while staying true to our founding promise: luxury quality, honest prices.

2019

Founded in a small studio. First 10 styles launched.

2020

3,000 customers. First sustainable capsule collection.

2022

20,000 customers. Artisan partnerships in Italy & India.

2024

50,000+ customers. 120+ styles. 35+ countries.
